Card

Use suiCard to group related content in a visual container. Cards support variants, sizes, body content, titles, and action areas.

Basic usage

The default card uses variant="border" and size="md".

Cards are useful for examples, summaries, grouped metadata, forms, and contextual actions.

Variants

Available variants are plain, border, and dash.

Plain

A simple card without border styling.

Border

The default Sushi card variant.

Dash

A dashed card for lightweight grouping.

Sizes

Available sizes are xs, sm, md, lg, and xl.

XS

Extra small card.

SM

Small card.

MD

Medium card.

LG

Large card.

XL

Extra large card.

Actions

Use suiCardActions for button rows and combine it with Tailwind utilities for alignment.
