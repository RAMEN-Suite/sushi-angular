Card

Use sui-card to group related content in a visual container. Cards support variants, sizes, media, titles, and action areas.

Basic usage

The default card uses variant="border" and size="md". Width remains a layout decision; this card is deliberately constrained.

Card title

Cards group a focused piece of content and its related actions.

 
<sui-card class="w-80 max-w-full">
  <h2 suiCardTitle>Card title</h2>
  <p>Card content</p>
</sui-card>

Variants

Available variants are plain, border, and dash.

Plain

A surface without a visible border.

Border

The default solid border.

Dash

A dashed border for secondary grouping.

 
<sui-card variant="plain">...</sui-card>
<sui-card variant="border">...</sui-card>
<sui-card variant="dash">...</sui-card>

Sizes

Sizes scale the card-owned padding, regular inherited text, and suiCardTitle. Explicit typography utilities still take precedence, and nested controls keep their own independent size API.

Extra small

Compact title, text, and spacing.

Small

Small title, text, and spacing.

Medium

The default scale.

Large

Large title, text, and spacing.

Extra large

The most generous scale.

 
<sui-card size="xs">...</sui-card>
<sui-card size="sm">...</sui-card>
<sui-card size="md">...</sui-card>
<sui-card size="lg">...</sui-card>
<sui-card size="xl">...</sui-card>

Constrained widths

Cards do not force fluid width. Use layout utilities to choose an exact or maximum width while retaining max-w-full for small screens.

Compact profile

A narrow card for short supporting content.

Detailed summary

A wider card provides room for longer descriptions without occupying the entire parent.

 
<sui-card class="w-64 max-w-full" size="sm">...</sui-card>
<sui-card class="w-96 max-w-full" size="lg">...</sui-card>

Fluid layouts

Use w-full when cards should fill their grid track or parent. The grid remains one column on mobile and expands when space is available.

Actions

Use suiCardActions on a native container. The directive adds the component's action alignment and spacing.

Order Summary

Design Service $120.00
Hosting $30.00
Total $150.00 
<sui-card class="w-full max-w-md">
  <h3 suiCardTitle>Order Summary</h3>
  <div suiDivider></div>
  <div suiCardActions>
    <button suiButton type="button">Pay</button>
  </div>
</sui-card>

Media

Use suiCardMedia on a native media container to project it outside the component-owned body.

Manuscript preview

Combine media, metadata, and actions with normal HTML and layout utilities.

 
<sui-card class="w-full max-w-sm overflow-hidden">
  <figure suiCardMedia>...</figure>
  <h3 suiCardTitle>Preview</h3>
  <div suiCardActions>...</div>
</sui-card>
