Avatar

Use suiAvatar for profile images, initials, and presence.

Basic usage

Place an image inside suiAvatarContent. Size and shape remain part of the SUSHI API. 

<div suiAvatar>
  <div suiAvatarContent size="xl"><img src="avatar.jpg" alt="Ada" /></div>
</div>

Custom sizes

Choose a preset from extra small through extra large.

<div suiAvatarContent size="xs">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent size="xl">...</div>

Shapes and masks

Use square and rounded frames or crop the image to a circle, squircle, or hexagon.

<div suiAvatarContent shape="square">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="circle">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="squircle">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="hexagon">...</div>

All masks

Masks provide decorative crops while the source image and accessible text remain unchanged. 

<div suiAvatarContent shape="heart">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="hexagon">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="decagon">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="pentagon">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="diamond">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="star">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="triangle">...</div>

Partial masks

Use maskHalf to render either half of a mask, for example in ratings or comparisons. 

<div suiAvatarContent shape="star" maskHalf="first">...</div>
<div suiAvatarContent shape="star" maskHalf="second">...</div>

Presence and placeholder

Presence communicates availability. Initials provide a fallback when an image is unavailable.

SU
<div suiAvatar status="online">...</div>
<div suiAvatar placeholder>...</div>

Avatar group

Group overlapping avatars and end with a placeholder for additional people.

+99
<div suiAvatarGroup class="-space-x-6">
  <div suiAvatar>...</div>
</div>
