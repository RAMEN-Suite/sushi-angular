Fieldset

Group controls that answer one shared question. The native fieldset and legend communicate that relationship to assistive technology.

Related choices

Use a fieldset when several controls belong to the same question. Each checkbox keeps its own label while the legend names the complete group.

Notification channels 
<fieldset suiFieldset class="border-base-300 rounded-box border px-4 pt-2 pb-4">
  <legend suiFieldsetLegend>Notification channels</legend>
  <label class="label">
    <input suiCheckbox type="checkbox" checked /> Email
  </label>
  <label class="label">
    <input suiCheckbox type="checkbox" /> Push notification
  </label>
  <label class="label">
    <input suiCheckbox type="checkbox" /> Text message
  </label>
</fieldset>

Collapsible fieldset

Add collapsible, a toggle inside the legend, and suiFieldsetContent. Plus and minus are supplied by default, while [(expanded)] can synchronize the state with a signal. 

expanded = signal(true);
 
<fieldset suiFieldset collapsible [(expanded)]="expanded">
  <legend suiFieldsetLegend>
    <button suiFieldsetToggle>Profile details</button>
  </legend>
  <div suiFieldsetContent>
    <label for="first-name">First name</label>
    <input suiInput class="w-full" id="first-name" />
    <label for="last-name">Last name</label>
    <input suiInput class="w-full" id="last-name" />
  </div>
</fieldset>

Custom collapse indicator

Place an element with sui-fieldset__icon before the title to replace the default indicator. This example rotates a Lucide chevron from the bound expanded state.

Time in minutes.

expanded = signal(false);
 
<fieldset suiFieldset collapsible [(expanded)]="expanded">
  <legend suiFieldsetLegend>
    <button suiFieldsetToggle>
      <svg lucideChevronRight
        class="sui-fieldset__icon size-4 transition-transform"
        [class.rotate-90]="expanded()" />
      Advanced settings
    </button>
  </legend>
  <div suiFieldsetContent>
    <label for="timeout">Session timeout</label>
    <input suiInput class="w-full" id="timeout" type="number" />
  </div>
</fieldset>

Form section and disabled group

A bordered fieldset can define a form section. The native disabled attribute disables all controls in the group.

Account credentials
Managed preferences 
<fieldset suiFieldset>
  <legend suiFieldsetLegend>Account credentials</legend>
  <label for="email">Email</label>
  <input suiInput class="w-full" id="email" type="email" />
  <label for="password">Password</label>
  <input suiInput class="w-full" id="password" type="password" />
</fieldset>
 
<fieldset suiFieldset disabled>
  <legend suiFieldsetLegend>Managed preferences</legend>
  <label><input suiCheckbox type="checkbox" /> Automatic updates</label>
  <label><input suiCheckbox type="checkbox" /> Preview releases</label>
</fieldset>

Buttons and external collapse controls

Actions can remain inside the group they belong to. Collapse controls may live outside and manage the fieldset through its bound expanded state.

Contact details 
<form (submit)="$event.preventDefault()">
  <fieldset suiFieldset>
    <legend suiFieldsetLegend>Contact details</legend>
    <label for="name">Name</label>
    <input suiInput class="w-full" id="name" />
    <label for="email">Email</label>
    <input suiInput class="w-full" id="email" type="email" />
    <div>
      <button suiButton type="reset" variant="text">Cancel</button>
      <button suiButton type="submit">Save contact</button>
    </div>
  </fieldset>
</form>
