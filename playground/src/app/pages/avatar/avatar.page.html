Avatar

Use sui-avatar for profile images, initials, and presence.

Basic usage

Project an image or fallback into the avatar. Size, shape, masks, and presence remain part of one API. 

<sui-avatar size="xl">
  <img src="avatar.jpg" alt="Ada" />
</sui-avatar>

Custom sizes

Choose a preset from extra small through extra large.

<sui-avatar size="xs">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar size="xl">...</sui-avatar>

Shapes and masks

Use square and rounded frames or crop the image to a circle, squircle, or hexagon.

<sui-avatar shape="square">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="circle">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="squircle">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="hexagon">...</sui-avatar>

All masks

Masks provide decorative crops while the source image and accessible text remain unchanged. 

<sui-avatar shape="heart">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="hexagon">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="decagon">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="pentagon">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="diamond">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="star">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="triangle">...</sui-avatar>

Partial masks

Use maskHalf to render either half of a mask, for example in ratings or comparisons. 

<sui-avatar shape="star" maskHalf="first">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar shape="star" maskHalf="second">...</sui-avatar>

Presence and placeholder

Presence communicates availability. Initials provide a fallback when an image is unavailable.

SU 
<sui-avatar status="online">...</sui-avatar>
<sui-avatar placeholder>...</sui-avatar>

Avatar group

Group overlapping avatars and end with a placeholder for additional people.

+99 
<div suiAvatarGroup class="-space-x-6">
  <sui-avatar>...</sui-avatar>
</div>
